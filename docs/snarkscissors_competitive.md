# SnarkScissors - Competitive Analysis
## Rock-Paper-Scissors Party Games on Steam

### Direct Competition - RPS Games

#### Rock Paper Scissors Party (Steam)
- **Type:** 3D multiplayer party game
- **Features:** MMO, LAN, PvP multiplayer
- **Status:** Recently released (2024)
- **Weaknesses:** Generic name, possible lack of polish
- **Strengths:** Already established on Steam

#### Rock Paper SMASH (Steam)
- **Type:** Platform fighter with RPS mechanics
- **Features:** Up to 4 players, online/local, single player adventure
- **Unique angle:** Combines RPS with platform fighting
- **Weaknesses:** More complex gameplay, may deter casual players
- **Strengths:** Innovative approach to RPS

#### Super RPS (Steam)
- **Type:** Tactical strategy RPS
- **Features:** 5 fields simultaneously, field cards, special abilities
- **Rating:** 78% positive (19 reviews)
- **Weaknesses:** Too complicated for casual audience
- **Strengths:** Depth for hardcore players

#### ShapeRockets (Steam)
- **Type:** FPS with RPS mechanics
- **Features:** Online multiplayer, minimalist design
- **Unique angle:** Literal RPS shooter
- **Weaknesses:** May be too abstract
- **Strengths:** Original concept

#### The RPS Game Show (Steam)
- **Type:** Game show format
- **Features:** Challenge friends, twist mechanics
- **Weaknesses:** May be one-time experience
- **Strengths:** Entertainment value

### Similar Competition - Party Games

#### Custom Party (Steam)
- **Type:** Free indie party game
- **Features:** Character customization, different powers/stats
- **Monetization:** Free-to-play
- **Inspiration:** Good example of customization + party gameplay

#### General party games with customization:
- Focus on character customization
- Multiplayer (local/online)
- Casual approach
- Often F2P model

### Market Gap Analysis

#### What's Missing in the Market:

**1. Polished RPS with Character Customization**
- Most RPS games are either too simple or too complicated
- **SnarkScissors opportunity:** Balanced complexity with deep customization

**2. Shake and Fidget Aesthetic for Multiplayer**
- Shake and Fidget style is popular, but mostly single-player
- **Opportunity:** Transfer this style into multiplayer environment

**3. Consistent Content Updates**
- Most RPS games have limited content
- **Opportunity:** Regular updates, seasonal events, cosmetics

**4. Community Features**
- Missing guilds, tournaments, user-generated content
- **Opportunity:** Strong community building

### Pricing & Monetization Insights

#### Competitive Pricing:
- **Premium games:** $9.99-19.99 (most RPS games)
- **F2P games:** Cosmetic microtransactions
- **Hybrid model:** Base game + DLC/cosmetics

#### Recommended Strategy:
- **Launch:** $9.99-12.99 (competitive with similar games)
- **Post-launch:** Cosmetic DLC $2.99-4.99
- **Season passes:** $9.99 quarterly

### Market Opportunities

#### Underserved Segments:

**1. Casual Multiplayer with Depth**
- Players want simple game but with long-term engagement
- **Solution:** RPS core + deep customization + community features

**2. Shake and Fidget Fanbase**
- Millions of players know and love this art style
- **Solution:** Transfer familiar style into new genre

**3. Content Creators**
- RPS games are perfect for streaming/YouTube
- **Solution:** Built-in sharing features, spectate mode, highlights

**4. Tournament Organizers**
- Dedicated tournament support is missing in RPS games
- **Solution:** Built-in tournament system, bracket management

### Competitive Threats

#### High Risk:
- **Copycats:** If we're successful, imitators will quickly appear
- **Mitigation:** Fast iteration, strong community, first-mover advantage

#### Medium Risk:
- **Big Studio Entry:** Large studio could make similar game with bigger budget
- **Mitigation:** Indie charm, community focus, agile development

#### Low Risk:
- **Platform Changes:** Steam policy changes
- **Existing Competitors:** Current competition isn't strong

### Strategic Recommendations

#### Immediate Actions:
1. **Differentiate Early** - Emphasize Shake and Fidget style in marketing
2. **Community Building** - Start Discord/social media before launch
3. **Content Creator Outreach** - Early access for streamers
4. **Polish Focus** - Competition often has low polish - this is our advantage

#### Launch Strategy:
1. **Soft Launch** - Early access with feedback loop
2. **Content Creator Beta** - Exclusive preview for influencers
3. **Steam Festivals** - Participate in Next Fest and similar events
4. **Viral Mechanics** - Built-in sharing, highlights, funny moments

#### Post-Launch:
1. **Regular Updates** - Monthly content drops
2. **Community Events** - Weekly tournaments, seasonal events
3. **User-Generated Content** - Workshop support, custom gestures
4. **Platform Expansion** - Mobile companion, web client

- Character customization is a popular feature
- Party games have a stable audience
- Shake and Fidget style is underutilized in multiplayer

#### Competitive Advantages:
- **Unique art style** (Shake and Fidget inspired)
- **Perfect complexity balance**
- **Community-first approach**

#### Success Factors:
- Polish over features
- Community engagement
- Regular content updates
- Strong visual identity
- Viral mechanics
